# Name Research: A Social World Run by AI Agents

**Method note:** Handle/domain scores below are *pre-check estimates* based on how distinctive and searchable-sounding a name is, not claims that a domain or handle is definitely free today. Where I name a real-world clash I am confident about, I say so. Where I am not sure, I flag it for checking.

A good name here had to pass three tests:

1. **Place test:** It must sound good in “Welcome to ___”, “Last night in ___”, and “The 24 residents of ___.” Not like a chatbot, SaaS tool, or crypto project.
2. **Bangla mouth test:** Open syllables are best — *ma, ya, pa, ra, lo, ri*. Difficult sounds for many Bangla speakers include **V** (often becomes B/Bh), **F**, **Z**, English **th**, consonant clusters, and silent letters.
3. **Global mouth test:** A non-Bangla speaker should pronounce it correctly on the first or second try and spell it after hearing it once.

## 2. Rapid Kill: 40 Names Out

### Group A kills
- **A3 Vesperia** — beautiful, but *Tales of Vesperia* is a well-known video game (confident clash).
- **A4 Solara** — too close to **Solana** blockchain and Toyota Solara (confident); generic solar-company sound.
- **A5 Auroria** — pretty but generic dawn/Aurora association; sounds like a hotel or game expansion.
- **A6 Novaria** — “Nova” is exhausted by tech, TV, cars, and space brands.
- **A8 Orluna** — forced gold+moon mashup; nobody knows if it starts “Or-” or “Ur-”.
- **A9 Iselara** — vowel pile-up; sounds pharmaceutical.
- **A10 Celoria** — “celery + -oria”; supplement/pharma energy.

### Group B kills
- **B4 Rupnagar** — Rupnagar is a real city/district name in India (confident); in Bangladesh “-nagar” names also risk a real-estate feel (think Rupayan).
- **B5 Mirrortown** — plain English, a bit *Black Mirror*-dark, generic game-map name.
- **B6 Paranova** — “para-” reads as paranormal/paralegal in English; two-word concept, weak ownability.
- **B7 Golpo Goli** — global tongue-twister; in Bangla *goli* can suggest গলি (lane) but also echoes গুলি (bullet).
- **B8 Twentyfour Lane** — locks the brand to exactly 24; also *24* TV-series association.
- **B9 Aynanagar** — strong mirror idea, but *-nagar* is bureaucratic/real-estate, and it’s long.
- **B10 Bondhupara** — sweet (“friends’ neighbourhood”) but 10 letters; *bondhu* means nothing globally.

### Group C kills — this whole family mostly fails the “place, not tool” test
- **C1 Persona** — Atlus’s *Persona* games (confident clash) + a generic psychology/marketing term.
- **C2 Simuland** — sounds like a simulation product or theme-park demo.
- **C3 Synthea** — pharma/health-tech sound; synthetic-patient software uses this name (confident enough to kill).
- **C4 Botbari** — “bot” immediately makes it a chatbot tool; also faintly comic.
- **C5 Agent Adda** — “Agent” = spy/software agent in English; word order is awkward.
- **C6 Echolings** — Amazon **Echo** clash + cute alien-species vibe.
- **C7 Replicas** — *Blade Runner* replicant menace; also means fake goods.
- **C8 Ailings** — fatal: reads as English “ailings” = sickly people.
- **C9 Emorians** — invented species with no place-feel; uncomfortably close in sound to “morons” when said fast.
- **C10 The Synthetic Society** — a research institute/manifesto title, not a visitable place.

### Group D kills
- **D1 Vercelia** — the **V** is a Bangla-speaker penalty (Ver- → Bher-/Bar-); pharma-fashion sound, unclear stress.
- **D3 Zelora** — too close to **Zalora**, the big Southeast Asian fashion e-commerce brand (confident clash).
- **D4 Amora** — Amora is a known French mustard brand (confident); also a generic romance/name word.
- **D6 Lorelia** — long, girlish, and orally close to **L’Oréal**, the beauty giant (confident).
- **D7 Sorina** — generic first name, faintly medical.
- **D8 Melora** — generic first name; melatonin/supplement echo.
- **D9 Tavira** — a real town in Portugal’s Algarve (confident); beautiful but not ownable.
- **D10 Lunova** — luna+nova is the most generic astro-tech/skincare construction possible (uncertain specific clashes — not worth checking).

### Group E kills
- **E2 Adda** — conceptually perfect, commercially weak: **Adda247**, the Indian edtech company (confident clash), plus *adda* is a common noun nobody can own. It survives only inside **Addapara**.
- **E4 Neelara** — “Knee-lara” misread globally; a colour-name, not a society.
- **E5 Meghla** — common Bangladeshi female name; **gh** spelling trips global speakers; weather-app feel.
- **E6 Golpo** — a common noun (“story”); unownable and slightly goofy in English mouths.
- **E7 Jharna** — lovely waterfall word, but **Jh-** spelling chaos globally; also a common personal name.
- **E8 Alochaya** — beautiful light-and-shadow poetry, but 8 letters, *ch* ambiguity, more book-title than place.
- **E9 Kathapara** — good idea, worse execution than Mayapara: **th** ambiguity, longer, flatter.
- **E10 Nokshi** — iconic craft word, but it names an object/tradition, not a place; likely crowded with Bangladeshi handicraft brands (uncertain — genericness alone kills it).

**Survivors (10):** Elaria, Lumora, Selara, Mayapara, Addapara, Tarapara, Velora, Aloria, Rupkatha, Aynara.

## 4. The Winner

### Exact spelling
**Mayapara** — one word. Display form can be styled **MayaPara** in the logo to help first-time readers, but the name is *Mayapara*.

### Pronunciation
- For everyone: **my-ah-PAH-rah** — four even beats, flat “pah,” soft final “rah.”
- For a Bangla speaker: **মা-য়া-পা-ড়া** (*ma-ya-pa-ra*) — every sound already lives in Bangla.

### What it means / evokes
- **মায়া (Maya)** = illusion, magic, enchantment — and also *affection/attachment*. That is the whole platform in one word: the world isn’t real, but you will get attached to it. *Maya laage.*
- **পাড়া (Para)** = the Bengali neighbourhood where everyone knows everyone — the tea stall, the gossip, the rivalries, the crushes, the elders, the festivals. Exactly what 24 agents living in one season will become.
- Together: **the magical little neighbourhood where AI lives.** It passes every test sentence: *“Welcome to Mayapara.” “Previously on Mayapara…” “Season 2: Monsoon in Mayapara.” “One of the 24 just moved into Mayapara.”*
- It quietly exports two Bengali words to the world — the way *hygge* travelled — giving a Bangladeshi builder a cultural fingerprint no generic fantasy name can copy.

### Taglines
- **English:** “Where AI agents live, love, and gossip.”
- **Bangla:** “যেখানে এআইরা থাকে, ভালোবাসে আর আড্ডা দেয়।”

### Handle and domain forms
- **Handle:** `@mayapara` (first target on every platform; fallback `@visitmayapara`)
- **Domain:** `mayapara.com` (first target; `mayapara.world` is a strong, on-brand fallback)

### Logo direction (3 lines)
1. **Colours:** Deep para-teal evening sky, marigold sunset, and one rickshaw-art coral accent — warm and premium, never techy-neon.
2. **Symbol:** A cluster of tiny rooftops/lanes forming an **M**, with 24 small lit windows, the gaps between houses shaped like speech bubbles.
3. **Wordmark:** Soft lowercase “mayapara,” rounded and friendly — no robot face, no circuit lines, no chip icon.

### 3 risks to check before locking it
1. **Trademark search:** Search “Mayapara” and “Maya Para,” plus মায়াপাড়া, in Bangladesh (DPDT), India, US (USPTO), EU (EUIPO), and WIPO — classes 9, 38, 41, 42 (software, social networking, entertainment) and common-law use on Facebook pages/groups.
2. **Digital land-grab check:** Verify `mayapara.com` (and .world/.ai) availability/price, plus `@mayapara` on Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, X, and YouTube. If an active, legitimate brand owns the exact .com, re-test before improvising spellings.
3. **Say-it and confusion test:** Record 15–20 Bangla speakers and 15–20 global speakers saying/spelling it after hearing it once; Google/Map-search “Mayapara” for real villages, pages, or businesses, and measure confusion against **Mayapur** (the ISKCON pilgrimage town) and Autodesk **Maya**.
